Revised SDRF Norms — Flood 2026

Office of the District Commissioner, Sivasagar · District Disaster Management Authority
ACTION REQUIRED

Letter Date
17-08-2026
Block Due Date

District Due Date
22-08-2026
Subject: Forwarding of revised SDRF Norms under Point No. 11 — Infrastructure (Repair/Restoration of damaged infrastructure) for information and necessary action.

Particular attention is invited to Point No. 11 (1), (2) & (4) — Infrastructure, covering immediate repair/restoration of damaged roads and bridges, drinking water supply schemes, culverts, power infrastructure, Primary/Community Health Centres and community assets, subject to the prescribed norms, ceilings and conditions.

Concerned authorities are requested to examine the infrastructure damaged during Flood 2026 and submit details of eligible repair/restoration works to the concerned Circle Office at Block level on or before 20-08-2026, so that the District-level submission can be made on or before 22-08-2026, along with the necessary assessment, estimates and supporting documents.

Key action: Ensure proposals strictly conform to the prescribed SDRF norms and relate only to eligible immediate repair/restoration of damaged infrastructure.
